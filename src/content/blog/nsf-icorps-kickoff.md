*AccelCorps's NSF I-Corps MVP pilot — Chicago kickoff, with Joe Cheng and NSF I-Corps Director Ruth Shuman (on screen).*

A group photo from the kickoff of a new NSF I-Corps pilot I'm advising. Run by **AccelCorps** and led by Joe Cheng, the four-week pilot on **MVP (Minimum Viable Product) Development and Testing** ran April 12 – May 8: a three-day in-person kickoff in Chicago, followed by three weekly Friday virtual sessions.

More than **70 national I-Corps teams applied, and 18 were selected** to take part. NSF I-Corps Director **Ruth Shuman** joined the opening session on screen.
